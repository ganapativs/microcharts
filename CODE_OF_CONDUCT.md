# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 3.0.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the microcharts community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to act in ways that build an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: showing empathy and kindness, respecting differing
opinions and experiences, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility and repairing
harm, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes: sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome advances; trolling, insulting or derogatory
comments, personal or political attacks; public or private harassment; publishing others' private information without
permission; and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at
**vsg.inbox@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated
to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

The full text of the standards, enforcement responsibilities, scope, and enforcement guidelines is the Contributor
Covenant v3.0, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/.
