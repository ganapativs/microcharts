DepthWedge answers "how much pressure is stacked on each side of the current level, and how wide is
the gap between them?". Demand accumulates leftward from the spread and supply rightward, so the two
filled wedges show the book's posture in a glyph. The y-scale is linear, full stop — log-depth
reading belongs to full-size tools.

## Why this default

Linear scale plus a stated `range` because a wedge you can't interrogate must not editorialize — a
silent log axis would exaggerate near-spread depth and flatter thin books. The visible range is part
of the claim, so the summary always carries "within the shown range", and the spread — the gap
between the best bid and best ask — is the headline number above the mid line.

## Accessibility

The accessible name states the posture, range-scoped — **"Demand outweighs supply 1.8× within the
shown range; spread 0.2."** The interactive entry walks the levels with ←/→, announcing the
cumulative depth on each side and its distance from the mid.
